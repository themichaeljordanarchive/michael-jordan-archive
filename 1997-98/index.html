The 1997–98 season represents the only documented instance in the archive where NBA Finals jerseys appear as distinct artifacts within the population.

  • Total Finals Artifacts: 6
  • Confirmed Population: 4
  • Publicly Documented: 2
  • Privately Verified: 2

A singular 1995–96 Chicago Bulls jersey preserved in the Smithsonian collection is known to have been worn during the 1996 NBA Finals. That garment represents a regular-season artifact that continued into postseason play rather than a distinct Finals-issued uniform.

Population conclusions reflect best-available documentation, comparative review, public sales history, and privately confirmed examples where ownership has requested anonymity.
