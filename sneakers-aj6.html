The

Air Jordan VI

1990-1991

Air Jordan V, designed by Tinker Hatfield, was introduced in early 1990 and worn by Michael Jordan during the 1989–90 NBA season. Internal production coding on early examples indicates manufacturing dating to August 1989, preceding the model’s on-court introduction and associated promotional imagery. Early appearances document initial production runs prior to full retail standardization, with minor variations observed across early-issued pairs.

Jordan first wore the Air Jordan V in February 1990, with rotation increasing through the latter half of the season and into the postseason. The model remained in consistent use during this period, with multiple pairs documented across regular season and playoff play, reflecting its role as the primary performance model through the conclusion of the 1989–90 campaign and start of the 1990-91 season.
