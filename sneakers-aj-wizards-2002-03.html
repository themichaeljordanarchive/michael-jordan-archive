The

Air Jordan Wizards 2002-03

2002–2003

The 2002–03 season reflects a multi-model rotation, with Michael Jordan wearing a combination of contemporary and retro footwear throughout his return with the Washington Wizards. Primary use centered around the Air Jordan XVIII, supported by intermittent appearances of the Air Jordan XVII and retro models including the Air Jordan III, Air Jordan VII, Air Jordan IX, and Air Jordan XI.

This rotation was influenced by performance preference, availability, and promotional considerations, resulting in varied use across the regular season. Photographic comparison and wear analysis indicate that multiple pairs and models were deployed interchangeably, requiring contextual placement rather than a single-model timeline to accurately document usage throughout the season.
