The Michael Jordan Archive

The Michael Jordan Archive is an independent research and documentation platform dedicated exclusively to the game-worn career of Michael Jordan.

The archive specializes in photo-matching, game attribution, and historical verification of Michael Jordan jerseys, sneakers, and related equipment. Research is grounded in primary photographic and video sources, supported by detailed comparative analysis of construction, tagging, wear patterns, and documented game context.

The archive is supported by a privately maintained collection of original photographic negatives acquired season-by-season, including imagery not publicly accessible through commercial databases or digital archives. In certain cases, these materials represent the only known visual documentation of specific game-worn garments.

Archive & Population Research

A core objective of The Michael Jordan Archive is the development of an evolving population record for Michael Jordan game-worn jerseys. This initiative seeks to bring structure and evidence-based documentation to a category historically lacking centralized archival research.

  • Identification and documentation of confirmed game-worn examples
  • Game-dating and season attribution by photographic record
  • Tracking confirmed and attributed garments across styles and years
  • Population analysis contextualizing scarcity and circulation

Research conducted through The Michael Jordan Archive supports collectors, auction houses, and institutions seeking rigorous, evidence-driven analysis. All findings are presented independently, with transparency regarding methodology and source limitations.
