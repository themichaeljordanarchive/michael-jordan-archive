The Sneaker Index reflects a different form of documentation than the Jersey Index. Because Michael Jordan wore new pairs at a far greater rate than jerseys, footwear identification often depends on more than direct visual comparison alone. This archive draws on long-term study of model chronology, production coding, material behavior, wear patterns, and player-specific characteristics observed across game photography and surviving examples.

Research note:Research note: The Sneaker Index is intended as a chronological footwear record rather than a population study. Because Michael Jordan rotated footwear at a far greater rate than jerseys, this section prioritizes documented public examples, observed use, photo matches, and verified coding characteristics associated with player-issued footwear. The research presented here reflects more than two decades of focused study in Michael Jordan game-worn sneakers, including model chronology, production traits, material analysis, wear behavior, and player-specific characteristics that distinguish authentic game use from superficial visual similarity.
