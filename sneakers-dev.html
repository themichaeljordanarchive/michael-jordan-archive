Sneaker Index

A chronological record of Michael Jordan’s game-worn footwear, organized by model and supported by documented public examples, photo matches, and classification through verified coding.

Methodology & Identification

Footwear identification within the archive extends beyond visual comparison. Because Michael Jordan rotated pairs at a far greater rate than jerseys, classification relies on model chronology, production characteristics, material behavior, and player-specific wear patterns observed across game imagery and surviving examples.

Final identification is based on the convergence of these factors, with emphasis placed on non-repeatable, pair-specific characteristics rather than isolated visual similarities.
