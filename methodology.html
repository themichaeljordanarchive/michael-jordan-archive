Scope & Methodology

Michael Jordan Archive documents and verifies Michael Jordan game-worn memorabilia using primary-source evidence and repeatable comparative analysis.

Scope

  • Chicago Bulls game-worn jerseys (home/away) by season and style
  • Game-used sneakers and related equipment
  • Publicly surfaced examples (auctions, private sales when documented)
  • Non-surfaced but documented use cases (noted as such)

Method

  • Comparative image analysis across multiple moments within the same game
  • Construction and tagging review where imagery allows
  • Wear-pattern continuity (repairs, marks, stitching, material behaviors)
  • Cross-referenced game logs, dates, opponent context, and uniform timelines

Negative Archive & Primary Sources

Research draws from a private archive of original photography and negatives, period publications, and broadcast footage, supplemented by additional contemporaneous sources when available. When a source is uncertain or incomplete, it is explicitly noted.

Population Research

The archive is building an evolving season-by-season population record for known Michael Jordan game-worn jerseys: documenting surfaced examples, tracking auction details when public, and noting confirmed use cases where no jersey has yet surfaced.

This archive does not speculate. Entries reflect confirmed evidence, stated uncertainty, or work in progress.
