Scope & Methodology

The Michael Jordan Archive documents and verifies game-worn memorabilia using primary-source evidence and repeatable comparative analysis. Where an item cannot be confirmed, uncertainty is stated explicitly.

Scope

  • Chicago Bulls game-worn jerseys (home/away) documented by season and style
  • Game-used sneakers and select related equipment when evidence allows
  • Publicly surfaced examples (auction, exhibition, or published documentation)
  • Documented use cases where no garment has publicly surfaced (explicitly noted)

Method

  • Comparative image analysis across multiple moments within the same game
  • Wear-pattern continuity review (repairs, marks, stitching, material behavior)
  • Construction and tagging review where imagery permits
  • Cross-referenced game dates, opponents, uniform timelines, and contextual sources

Primary Sources

Research draws from a private archive of original photography and negatives, period publications, and broadcast footage, supplemented by additional contemporaneous sources when available. When a source is incomplete, uncertain, or cannot be verified, it is noted accordingly.

Population Research

The archive maintains an evolving season-by-season population record for known Michael Jordan game-worn jerseys. This includes tracking publicly surfaced examples, documenting sale or exhibition details when available, and recording privately verified examples when ownership requests non-disclosure.

Confirmed population totals may include privately held examples not publicly disclosed. Public examples reflect items that have surfaced through sale, exhibition, or documented publication.

This archive does not speculate. Entries reflect confirmed evidence, stated uncertainty, or work in progress.
