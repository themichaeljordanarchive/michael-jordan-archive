1988 McDonalds All American Jersey

Publicly documented artifact.

Archive ReferenceJersey D
Confirmed Population1
Public Examples1
UsagePromotional
Research StatusComplete
Worn during a photoshoot in summer of 1987 for a promotional campaign surrounding the upcoming McDonalds All American Game.

Research & Provenance

This jersey was part of a yearly promotional event hosted by McDonalds, a single jersey was presented to Michael Jordan yearly for his partnership and promotion leading into the event. This particular jersey was mis-catgorized being a 1985 and a possible back up to his game worn, Jordan did not play in this yearly game since 1981 but, still received a Jersey in his specs yearly, confimred by our independent archive and press photos.
