The

Air Jordan XI

1995–1996

Air Jordan XI, designed by Tinker Hatfield, was introduced during the postseason of the 1994–95 NBA season and worn by Michael Jordan following his return to play. Early examples associated with this initial release include white/black pairs and black/concord pairs featuring “45” stitched on the heel, reflecting Jordan’s jersey number at the time. These pairs represent pre-release and early production forms of the model prior to full standardization.

In the 1995–96 season, the Air Jordan XI entered broader release with standardized production specifications, including the transition to “23” heel numbering and consistent model variations. Additional early examples are documented outside of game use, including a black concord pair worn during the filming of Space Jam, predating the 1994–95 playoff introduction and featuring “23” stitched on the heel. These variations collectively illustrate the model’s development from early sample and transitional forms into its established retail and in-game configurations.

Unlike jerseys, footwear is documented by model, use, and method of verification rather than population accounting.

Methodology & Identification

Footwear identification within the archive extends beyond direct visual comparison. Analysis incorporates model chronology, production coding, material behavior, and player-specific wear patterns observed across game imagery and surviving examples.

Particular attention is given to structural deformation during use, including creasing patterns, lace tension habits, and outsole or mid-sole stress responses resulting from Jordan’s movement and play style.

Final identification is determined through the convergence of these characteristics rather than any single matching detail.
