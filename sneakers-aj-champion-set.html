The

Air Jordan Championship Set

1991–1998

The championship set consists of six individual shoes associated with Michael Jordan’s title-winning games, each tied to a Bulls championship season. Following each championship victory, one shoe from the game was retained by Jordan, while its counterpart was given to Bulls PR director Tim Hallam, resulting in the intentional separation of each pair over time.

Through photographic comparison, wear characteristics, and construction analysis, each example is placed within its respective championship context. Viewed collectively, the surviving shoes form a complete record of Jordan’s footwear across all six title runs, illustrating both continuity of use and material variation during the defining stretch of his career.
