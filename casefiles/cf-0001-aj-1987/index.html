1986–87 Michael Jordan — Air Jordan Sneakers

Chicago Bulls vs. Milwaukee Bucks

Findings Summary

Comparative image analysis identified multiple consistent wear characteristics between the examined sneakers and documented in-game photography.

  • Midfoot lace stress distortion consistent across frames
  • Collar stitching irregularity matched to game imagery
  • Creasing pattern consistent with documented date span
