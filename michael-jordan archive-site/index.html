Research & Authentication

Michael Jordan Archive is an independent research and documentation platform dedicated exclusively to the game-worn career of Michael Jordan.

The archive specializes in photo-matching, game attribution, and historical verification of Michael Jordan jerseys, sneakers, and related equipment. All research is grounded in primary photographic and video sources, supported by detailed comparative analysis of construction, tagging, wear patterns, and period context.

In parallel with individual research projects, the archive is actively building a long-term reference resource for Michael Jordan game-worn jerseys. This work is designed to document known examples, establish game-dating timelines, and better understand season-by-season usage, reuse, and scarcity.

Archive & Population Research

A core objective of Michael Jordan Archive is the development of an evolving population report for Michael Jordan game-worn jerseys. This effort seeks to bring structure and transparency to a category that has historically lacked centralized, evidence-based documentation.

  • Identification and documentation of known game-worn jerseys
  • Game-dating and wear-period attribution by season
  • Tracking confirmed and attributed examples across styles and years
  • Population analysis to contextualize scarcity and circulation

Research conducted through Michael Jordan Archive supports collectors, auction houses, and institutions seeking rigorous, evidence-driven analysis. All findings are presented independently, with transparency regarding sources, methodology, and limitations.
